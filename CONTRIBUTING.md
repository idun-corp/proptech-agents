# Contributing to Proptech Agents

Thanks for your interest in contributing!

We welcome contributions from proptech professionals, AI developers, and partners who want to improve or extend the prompt collections.

---

## How to contribute

The prompts in `/embodied`, `/experts` and `/skills` are generated from ProptechOS's internal prompt store, so a pull request that edits them would be overwritten by the next generation run.

Instead, **open an Issue** describing the new agent or the change you'd like - the prompt text, the intent and context, and any safety guidance. We review it and publish it through the store.

### Guidelines

Keep prompts clear, safe, and purposeful.

Avoid including private or confidential data.

Attribution is handled under the CC BY 4.0 license.

All contributions are assumed to be made under the same license.

### Communication

For questions, feedback, or ideas — please open an Issue in this repository.
